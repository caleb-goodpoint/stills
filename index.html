Opening your library…

Caleb’s stills library. Sign in with Dropbox to open it; your frames are read straight from your Dropbox.

  1. In the Dropbox page that opened, tap Allow.
  2. Copy the code it shows.
  3. Come back to this app (tap Done, or open Stills from your home screen) and paste it below.
Only someone signed in to Caleb’s Dropbox can open this library. The page itself holds no frames.
